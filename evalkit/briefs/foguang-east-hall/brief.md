Design and build, from nothing, the East Main Hall of Foguang Temple. There is no model of it to
measure or copy: this brief, the photograph photo.jpg (shown to you now, and set above your hall in
brief.png each time you check it against this brief) and what your tools print are all you have.

What is known of the hall:
- It was built in 857, in the Tang dynasty, on a slope of Mount Wutai in Shanxi, China, where
  winters bring snow and the ground is known for earthquakes.
- It is a single-storey timber hall on a stone platform, seven bays across the front and four bays
  deep: 34 m from the first column to the last across the front, and 17.7 m deep.
- It stands on two rings of columns: an outer ring around the hall and an inner ring around its
  central space.
- Large bracket sets sit on every column head, about half as high as the column itself, and carry
  deep eaves.
- It has a single hipped roof with gentle slopes.

Hand the hall over only when it stands up to its own weight, snow and an earthquake, and answers
this brief.
